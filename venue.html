Getting to Melbourne

Melbourne is the capital of Victoria and one of Australia's major gateways, with excellent public transport and a compact, walkable city centre served by a free tram zone.

  • By air: Melbourne Airport (Tullamarine, MEL) is about 25 km from the city centre.
  • Public transport: trains, trams, and buses run on the myki card system; the CBD has a Free Tram Zone.
  • Accommodation: a range of hotels is available across the city centre and surrounds. Recommended options will be listed closer to the event.
