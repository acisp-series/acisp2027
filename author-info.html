Author Information

Camera-ready instructions for authors of accepted papers.

Coming soon. Detailed camera-ready instructions will be published here after author notification. The summary below outlines what to expect.

Registration requirement

Authors of accepted papers must guarantee that at least one of the authors will register and attend the conference and present their paper in-person. See the registration page for author registration deadlines.

Proceedings

The proceedings will be published by Springer as a volume of the Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series. Camera-ready papers must follow the LNCS format. Author instructions and LaTeX/Word templates are available from Springer's conference proceedings guidelines.

Camera-ready checklist

  • Prepare the final version using the Springer LNCS template.
  • Complete and sign the Springer copyright form (link provided after acceptance).
  • Address reviewer comments and ensure the paper stays within the page limit.
  • Submit the source files and PDF by the camera-ready deadline (see important dates).

For any questions about camera-ready submission, please contact the organisers.
