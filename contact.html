Invitation letters

If you require an official invitation letter for visa purposes, please email the organisers with the subject “ACISP Invitation Letter Request” and include:

  • Full name (as per your passport)
  • Date of birth and nationality
  • Affiliation and mailing address
  • Phone number (with country/area code)
  • Paper title (if applicable)
