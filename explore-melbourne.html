Explore Melbourne

Melbourne is consistently ranked among the world's most liveable cities — known for its laneways, coffee culture, arts, and sport.

Melbourne

Highlights

City & laneways

Explore the CBD's hidden laneways, street art, arcades, and rooftop bars. The city centre is compact and walkable, with a Free Tram Zone.

Arts & culture

Visit the National Gallery of Victoria, the Melbourne Museum, and the arts precinct along Southbank and the Yarra River.

Food & coffee

Melbourne's dining scene spans Queen Victoria Market, Chinatown, Lygon Street, and a world-renowned specialty coffee culture.

Sport

Home of the Australian Open, the Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG), and Grand Prix — a genuine sporting capital.

Day trips

The Great Ocean Road, the Yarra Valley wine region, the Dandenong Ranges, and Phillip Island are all within easy reach.

Getting around

Trams, trains, and buses run on the myki card. The CBD Free Tram Zone covers most central attractions.
